FP&A Financial Analysis Projects

FP&A projects centered on budgeting, forecasting, and financial analysis. These projects modeled corporate finance processes for business analysis.

Projects Included:
Annual Budget Model: Created a financial budget for a full year projecting revenues, expenses, and profitability for key segments of a business.
Forecasting and Scenario Analysis: Created financial forecasts and scenarios to analyze revenue growth changes of ±10-15% and cost changes.
Variance Analysis: Analyzed budget vs. actuals to identify key financial variances and performance drivers.
FP&A Dashboard: Created a financial dashboard to summarize key performance indicators, revenue growth, and key performance metrics.
